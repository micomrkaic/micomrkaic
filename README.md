## Hi, I'm Mico

I'm an economist who writes numerical software, mostly array languages,
calculators and simulations, and almost all of it in plain C. I looked at
OCaml and Go; nothing appears to beat good old C.

Most projects here follow the same rules: C17 or C23, a Makefile, few or no
dependencies, a test suite, and a manual. Many also compile to WebAssembly,
so you can try them in the browser without installing anything.

### Languages and calculators

- **[cozy](https://github.com/micomrkaic/cozy)** is a functional matrix
  language with Octave-flavoured syntax: dual numbers for exact
  differentiation, sparse matrices, pipelines, and three interchangeable
  linear-algebra backends.
  [Try it in the browser](https://micomrkaic.github.io/cozy/).
- **[MM-41](https://github.com/micomrkaic/MM-41)** is a programmable RPN
  calculator for the terminal, named after the HP-41. It handles real,
  complex and exact decimal scalars, matrices, user-defined words, and a
  finance module that computes in 34-digit decimals.
- **[Neutrino](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Neutrino)** is the array
  language that came before cozy. Every Neutrino program is still a valid
  cozy program.

### Tools for economists

- **[tea](https://github.com/micomrkaic/tea)**, the tiny econometric
  assistant, covers the data-prep work economists use Stata for, with
  syntax close to Stata's. It reads and writes `.dta`, runs do-files, and
  estimates OLS and panel models with robust and clustered errors.
  [Try it in the browser](https://micomrkaic.github.io/tea/).
- **[Kalman-Smoother](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Kalman-Smoother)** is a
  teaching package that writes the Kalman smoother as one sparse linear
  system instead of a forward-backward recursion, in Python and
  MATLAB/Octave, for graduate students and applied macroeconomists.

### Sound and pictures

- **[audiotard](https://github.com/micomrkaic/audiotard)** adds pleasant
  distortion to music (tube, tape, vinyl), calibrated in physical units,
  then runs blind listening tests to find out whether you can hear it.
- **[tagplay](https://github.com/micomrkaic/tagplay)** is a terminal music
  player that searches your library like a dataset: type a query and
  watch the match count update on every keystroke.
- **[noise-suite](https://github.com/micomrkaic/noise-suite)** synthesises
  sleep sounds: coloured noise, rain, sea, wind, crickets.
- **[polajuice](https://github.com/micomrkaic/polajuice)** turns clean
  digital photos and video into modelled film processes: camera, emulsion,
  developing, age.

### Physics you can watch

- **[pond](https://github.com/micomrkaic/pond)** is a wave tank with
  linear dispersive water waves solved spectrally and advanced exactly in
  time, drawn in 3-D with refraction and caustics.
- **[Ising-and-Potts-Models](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Ising-and-Potts-Models)**
  runs multithreaded Metropolis and Wolff Monte Carlo with jackknife error
  bars.
- **[Solar-System](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Solar-System)** is an
  N-body simulation with a symplectic integrator and JPL Horizons initial
  conditions.
- **[wind-tunnel-2d](https://github.com/micomrkaic/wind-tunnel-2d)** is a
  molecular-dynamics wind tunnel: a gas of soft discs flowing past an
  obstacle.
- **[Verlet-2Gasses-Simulation](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Verlet-2Gasses-Simulation)**
  is a hard-sphere gas showing thermalisation, equipartition and the
  H-theorem.
  [Run it in the browser](https://micomrkaic.github.io/Verlet-2Gasses-Simulation/).
- Smaller ones:
  [Phonons](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Phonons),
  [Coupled-Springs-2D](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Coupled-Springs-2D),
  [Quantum-Bouncing-Ball](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Quantum-Bouncing-Ball),
  [Earth-Satellites](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Earth-Satellites).

### Everyday tools and games

- **[soon](https://github.com/micomrkaic/soon)** is a keyboard-driven
  agenda and todo list for the terminal. Everything in it will be done
  soon.
- **[lunar-lander](https://github.com/micomrkaic/lunar-lander)** stars
  Eagle the Fat Cat, a very round cat with a rocket strapped to his
  backside.
- **[carpet_defense](https://github.com/micomrkaic/carpet_defense)** is a
  Space Invaders variant in which you defend a carpet from falling drinks.
  [Play it in the browser](https://micomrkaic.github.io/carpet_defense/).
- **[Ice-Burger-Blocks](https://github.com/micomrkaic/Ice-Burger-Blocks)**
  is a falling-blocks game made of ice cream and burgers, scored in
  calories.
